## B. Repetition thinning — proposed standing policy (the koa precedent, generalized)

219 gates carry repetition flags across ~30 courses — too many for family-by-family rounds.
Proposed policy, applied per course during Phase 4 with every affected family named in that
course's diff:

   identical-reveal triplet, tf-03's, se-01-01/02): thin to one canonical anchor (the strongest
   per the adjudication notes — typically the one whose reveal does the most work), REMOVE the
   copies, transplanting any unique good clause into the canonical's rewrite, exactly as your

2. **Cross-course duplicates** (ks-03-01 ≡ smg1-03-01, K vs G1; tg-03-01 ≡ ti-01-01): KEEP both
   — different courses serve different learners; meeting the idea again in a later course is
   spaced reinforcement, not redundancy.
3. **Spaced pairs within a course** (same claim, different numbers, different lessons — e.g.
   sy-03-01/-03): KEEP both — plausibly intentional reinforcement, and removal saves little.
