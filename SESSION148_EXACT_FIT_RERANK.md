# Session 148 exact-fit rerank

## Decision

Selected `exactNumberLab`, a seven-lesson closure spanning 51 authored experiences: 48 conversions and three retained direct interactions. The live queue moved **17 → 10**.

The closure was accepted only because one normalized exact-number state can derive grouping results, rational arithmetic, powers, inequalities, fraction benchmarks, root classification, and root bracketing without reducing the learner action to a generic calculator.

## Accepted lessons
- `fa-02-02` — Fraction Benchmark Comparisons: Exact rational comparison and benchmark-side reasoning share the same normalized value/order state.
- `dop-01-02` — Grouping Symbols First: Grouping order, intermediate subexpression value, and final exact evaluation are one expression-state derivation.
- `ee-01-02` — Powers: Power evaluation and comparison are exact-number construction and ordering tasks.
- `ee-05-01` — Inequalities: Boundary membership and extremum claims derive from exact order and inclusivity.
- `rno-04-03` — Rational Operations: Signed rational operations remain exact fractions/integers under the same value model.
- `rns-02-01` — Root Classification: Root values, perfect-square status, rationality, and ordering derive from exact surd states.
- `rns-02-03` — Root Density and Bracketing: Root bracketing, density witnesses, and placement derive from exact order bounds.

## Deferred queue

The following ten lessons remain classified because their learner actions require spatial construction, data-distribution manipulation, or geometry-specific invariants rather than exact-number ordering:
- `kc-02-03` — Order the Numbers: `dragOrder; numberLinePlace`. Classified from the authored prompts. Acceptance contract: Does richer causality improve ordering, or merely inflate the rubric?
- `md-05-02` — The Missing Side: `barBuilder or perimeterConstraintLab`. Classified from the authored prompts. Acceptance contract: Known sides and leftover perimeter must be spatially visible; rectangle pair constraint must be enforced.
- `cg-01-03` — Graphs That Tell Stories: `plotPoint/covariationScrubber coordinate-story mode`. Classified from the authored prompts. Acceptance contract: The coordinate or graph named in the prompt must exist on screen.
- `asv-03-03` — Coordinate Plane Capstone: `compositeAreaLab + plotPoint/coordinate mode`. Classified from the authored prompts. Acceptance contract: Coordinates must generate actual side lengths and attached/cut pieces.
- `dd-01-01` — Questions That Expect Variety: `dragBucket evidence mode`. Classified from the authored prompts. Acceptance contract: Classification may be the true skill; do not invent manipulation unrelated to variability.
- `dd-04-01` — Range: How Far Data Stretches: `dotPlot/boxPlot range mode`. Classified from the authored prompts. Acceptance contract: Min and max must be marked while interior changes demonstrate range blindness.
- `g7-01-03` — How Area Scales: `dilationExplore + area readout`. Classified from the authored prompts. Acceptance contract: Learner must see lengths scale by k while area scales by k².
- `g7-03-02` — Vertical and Adjacent Angles: `angleCrossingLab/angleMeasure`. Classified from the authored prompts. Acceptance contract: Vertical equality and adjacent supplement must update together around a real crossing.
- `tm-03-03` — Angle-Angle Similarity: `triangleAngleLab paired-triangle mode`. Classified from the authored prompts. Acceptance contract: Two matching angles must force the third; scale factor belongs only after similarity is established.
- `tm-04-01` — Why the Theorem Works: `pythagoreanAreaLab/shapeParts`. Classified from the authored prompts. Acceptance contract: Squares on the sides must show area conservation, not only a formula reveal.

## Rejected subclosures

A two-lesson root-only lab was rejected as an unnecessarily narrow subclosure. A generic calculation engine was also rejected because it would erase grouping, boundary, classification, and density distinctions.
