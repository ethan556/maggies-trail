# Advanced manipulative audit — Precalculus and Calculus, Session 96

## Audit decision

The combined audit reviewed every interactive surface in the eight Precalculus and eight Calculus courses and distinguished response/reveal interactions from genuine mathematical manipulatives.

- **199** advanced interactive surfaces;
- **82** true mathematical manipulatives;
- **82/82** explicitly CML-wired;
- **16** flagships, one per course;
- **66** supporting wires;
- **three** new engine types;
- **four** exact passive/recognition replacements.

## Why only three new engines

The audit used a causal sufficiency test: create a new engine only when no existing widget lets the learner change the governing quantity, see the linked consequence, test a misconception, and produce evidence of understanding. Under that standard, most existing advanced engines were mathematically strong but under-wired.

### New engines

1. `conicLocusLab` — exposes the unified focus–directrix ratio and eccentricity boundaries.
2. `derivativeRuleLab` — derives product and chain rules from changing quantities rather than revealing formulas.
3. `relatedRatesLab` — couples geometry and rates in a live constraint system.

## Flagship architecture

Each of the 16 courses has one flagship interaction with the complete predict–manipulate–observe–explain–revise–translate–fade–transfer–retrieve sequence. Supporting manipulatives retain shorter lesson pacing but now declare the same invariant and misconception architecture.

## Quality judgment

No additional engine is currently justified merely to increase widget count. High-value next additions should be evidence-led and should address a missing causal mechanism, such as three-dimensional multivariable surfaces or formal epsilon–delta control, only when those topics enter the authored curriculum. For the current course content, reuse plus complete wiring gives greater mastery value than proliferating near-duplicate widgets.

## Result

**82/82 direct advanced manipulatives pass the explicit CML coverage audit.**
