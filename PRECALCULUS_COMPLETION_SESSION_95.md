### New `conicLocusLab`

The existing graph and transformation engines could display conics but could not make the shared focus–directrix law causally visible. `conicLocusLab` lets learners vary eccentricity, sample both sides of the boundary, observe bounded/unbounded locus behavior, and land on the parabola threshold at e = 1. It replaces a recognition-only MCQ in `co-05-02#i1`.

The remaining Precalculus engines were retained because they already support high-value actions: zooming and asymptotic behavior, secant-rate comparison, unit-circle motion, complex-plane vectors, polar tracing, matrix transformations, and vector decomposition. Their deficiency was deployment and explicit learning structure, not their mathematical behavior.
