## Why every lesson remains REVISE / visual REQUIRED

Every lesson has one remedial route. In all 14:

1. the remedial concept has no figure, diagram, number line, bar, or manipulative even though the main lesson already demonstrates that the concept is better taught visually; and
2. the remedial check shares its evaluator/result with one or more main checks and replays the same mathematical job with additional directions.

This is particularly harmful on a retry path: a learner who did not succeed with the main sequence receives less representation and then essentially the same question. The bounded implementation requirement is to give each remedial concept a synchronized semantic representation and replace its check with a misconception-specific transfer task. Stable IDs and evaluator truth should be preserved where possible, but the new task must not repeat the original quantity and job.

Representative examples:

- `g5u-01-02` returns to `1/2 = ?/6`, already used by k1 and k3.
- `g5u-01-05` and `g5u-02-01` repeat their k1 jug arithmetic with extra counting/cross-out directions but no supplied model.
- `g5u-02-04` asks the learner to draw the regrouping model the visual-first app should provide, then repeats k1/ch1's legal-trade reasoning.
- `g5u-03-01` withholds a benchmark line while returning to the same `7/8 + 1/12` estimate used throughout the main route.
- `g5u-03-03` explicitly tells the learner to sketch the three-sixths-plus-two-sixths model and repeats k1's result.

No lesson is escalated: each residual cause is local, deterministic, and implementable with existing fraction/number-line figure families and current evaluator patterns.
