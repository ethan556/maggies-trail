## What the 48 P0 rows actually mean

The P0 volume is real, but it is generated by two shared causes:

1. **Thirty-two figure rows:** the builder hard-codes `figure: "count-on-hops"` onto both concept steps in every lesson. The fixed-exemplar alignment guard correctly suppresses all 32 because that asset depicts a specific 4 + 3 hop state and does not match any of these counting concepts. The lesson body therefore becomes text-first at both concept moments.
2. **Sixteen progression rows:** the assembly clones the same i1 widget into i2 and requires at least one `numberLineHop` per lesson to satisfy a legacy tier score. That mechanical recipe creates exact repeats and introduces unrelated number-line jobs even where the mathematical evidence should be objects, marks, direction, rearrangement, paired groups, a source pile, dice patterns, or a ten-frame.

Closing the three assessor decisions does not implement either cause. The 48 P0 rows remain open until runtime-visible representations and distinct question jobs are verified.

## Release-blocking semantic defect

`khm-03-05` and `khm-03-06` inherit `successFeedback` and `missFeedback` from a corpus flash template without rewriting them after the builder changes `count`. Consequently:

- `khm-03-05/k2` has `count: 3` but says “Watch again and count: 5 dots” and “Yes — 5!”;
- `khm-03-05/ch1` has `count: 4` but gives the same count-5 feedback;
- `khm-03-06/k2` and `khm-03-06/ch1` both have `count: 4` but give count-5 feedback.

The current `src/lib/g0Variants.ts` shared `subitize` helper derives feedback from `count`; the stale course builder’s local `flash` helper does not. This is a learner-visible false mathematical statement and must block release independently of the queue’s heuristic classifications.
