1. **Visual promise mismatch**: The lesson title ("Tenths on a Number Line"), c1 body, and c2 body describe a 0-to-1 number-line walk, but `c1.figure` is `"dpv-hundredths-grid"` and every interactive widget (`i1`, `i2`) is `"type": "hundredthsGrid"` — the same strip/grid used for the unrelated tenths-intro lessons. Either (a) swap the figure/widget to an actual number-line component that renders 0, the ten tick marks, and the target point synchronized to the prompt (preferred, matches the title's promise), or (b) if a number-line widget genuinely does not exist yet for this concept, rewrite c1/c2/title/predict copy to stop promising a number-line and describe the grid model actually shown. Do not leave the current mismatch (title says number line, widget renders grid).
2. **Grammar defect** (isolated to `ch1`, the only lesson-wide instance of this pattern — confirmed via full-repo grep): fix singular/plural agreement wherever the shaded count is 1:
   - `ch1.explanationVariants[0]`: `"1 shaded columns of 10 is 1 tenths: 0.1."` → `"1 shaded column of 10 is 1 tenth: 0.1."`
   - `ch1.explanationVariants[1]`: `"Each column is one tenth, so 1 columns writes as 0.1."` → `"...so 1 column writes as 0.1."`
   - `ch1.widget.commonErrors[0].feedback`: `"...so 1 columns is 1 tenths, written..."` → `"...so 1 column is 1 tenth, written..."`
   - `ch1.widget.commonErrors[1].feedback`: `"...would mean 1 cells of a hundred-cell grid."` → `"...would mean 1 cell of a hundred-cell grid."`
